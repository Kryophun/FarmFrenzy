WELCOME TO FARM FRENZY

Room for two.
Territory for one.

Two people play on the same device. Fern starts, then take turns selecting one of your pieces and an empty destination.

  1. Clone: move one square horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Your original stays put.
  2. Jump: move to any square exactly two spaces away. Your original square empties; you can jump over pieces.
  3. Flip: all opposing pieces touching your destination become yours, including diagonally.

If you have no legal move, your turn passes automatically. The game ends when the board is full, a player has no pieces, or neither player can move. Most pieces wins; equal scores are a draw.

Keyboard: Tab to the board, arrow keys to explore, Enter or Space to select and move. Escape clears your selection.
